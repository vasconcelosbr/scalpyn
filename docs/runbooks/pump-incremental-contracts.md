# Pump incremental labels and frozen research contract

The six horizons are enqueued atomically with the first immutable observation.
Queue identity is owner/observation/frozen label hash/horizon. A partial index
orders mature work by ready time; completed pairs are never reread or relabeled.
Migration233 backfills only missing pairs without rewriting historical labels.
The owner advisory transaction lock prevents competing consumers. Completion,
labels and job evidence commit together; timeout rolls them all back.

The current dedicated Pump worker retains its existing30s cycle. Each job has
one collective read of distinct instrument/minute revisions and one atomic
insert, rather than queries per pair. Default512 pairs means a theoretical
1024/min ceiling, above the306/min generated by51 instruments x six horizons
and the600/min max admitted by the100-asset capture budget. That is a demand
calculation, not a substitute for runtime proof. Time stays4s, SQL3s, read
100000 price points/20MB encoded JSON; raw storage stays1GB. A read-budget
exhaustion preserves pending work and reports failure; it never manufactures
unknown/loss outcomes or retries in an unlimited loop. UI/API display due,
waiting, oldest lag and measured job throughput. Capture does not await labels.

Before deployment, real PostgreSQL TEMP fixture:51 instruments x2 observations
x6 horizons,6222 paths spanning122minutes. Two jobs drain612pairs:512 in3023ms,
100 in1847ms; due queue zero, production writes zero. Real production must
demonstrate backlog decreasing and then remaining bounded while capture grows.
No capacity claim is made for arbitrary future source density above read caps.

## Identity and training manifest

`pump_contracts.gate_listing_record` uses public Gate pair/base/quote and the
provider buy/sell-start epoch. Nonzero, valid provider dates and a tradable pair
are required; zero dates are unverified. Source capture time/evidence hash are
preserved. A changed epoch changes instrument/episode. Old observations stay
uncertified; no retrospective enrichment or invented exchange listing history.
Evidence should be refreshed/reviewed before a training export; the public pair
identifier alone cannot detect all unannounced token migrations/relistings.

Feature dictionary `pump_numeric_features_v1` fixes names, units and capture
availability semantics. A model cohort must share dictionary, producer config,
label specification, reference and cost hashes. `freeze_pump_manifest` validates
explicit feature selection, exact label/cost definitions, three strict temporal
cuts, >=120min embargo, independent support criteria and resource ceilings.
Dates/support thresholds are explicit research choices; no new statistical
minimum or trading gate is silently activated. Costs remain explicitly null
until measured/approved: target is gross and net is not fabricated. The trainer
validates this complete manifest before heavy imports and stores its immutable
hash alongside the native Booster/calibrator. Promotion/inference/delta stay off.

## Risk and statistical presentation

Unconfigured spread/slippage/extension limits are explicit. Reaching the point
limit is displayed as confirmations, never economic suitability. Slippage
is buy VWAP minus mid, in percent of mid, for the captured legacy reference
notional; it already includes displacement from mid and excludes fees. Do not
add spread to it or interpret it as slippage relative to the best ask.
All exploratory frequencies keep denominator and unvalidated-support warning;
2/4 or0/1 are not reliable patterns or probabilities.

For a later explicit decision, possible provisional risk options are:
reuse existing legacy red slippage0.20% with max spread0.50%; or a tighter
spread0.20%/slippage0.20% (each one quarter of the0.80% gross target). Neither
option is calibrated, activated or recommended as economically validated.
Extension and risk penalties also require a recorded decision; no legacy
Pool/exit thresholds change as part of that choice.

## Exclusive daily training job — approval updated

The user explicitly confirmed1vCPU/2GB, one daily run <=15min, billing by use
without a guaranteed monthly monetary cap. No workspace limit is changed.
Cron06:00UTC avoids the existing weekly03:00 trainer slot; one replica, no
restart/retry, database singleton and one execution per UTC date. Coreutils
TERM895s/KILL5s bounds process lifetime to900s. The image has independently
pinned dependencies and copies only three pure Pump modules; no Shadow model,
task, queue or artifact module is included. Existing DB binding is reused;
there is no new credential or bucket. Native model/calibrator/manifest/metrics
are preserved in owner-scoped Pump registry/artifact tables, with5MB ceiling.

Mechanical challenger floors are explicit and provisional:200 compatible rows,
100 episodes,10 instruments,1day; each purged temporal cohort needs both target
outcomes. Cuts are50/70/85% of distinct captured times, with120min embargo and
max horizon purge. Max10000 rows/one thread/100 depth3 trees. These are compute
eligibility choices, not evidence of statistical model readiness; no trained
challenger is promoted or contributes score. Insufficient data produces a
registered blocked run and clean exit. A native monetary per-service hard cap
has not been verified; runtime ceilings do not guarantee an invoice amount.

Listing metadata refresh is a bounded public read every6h on the existing Pump
queue, max10 owners/100 instruments,20s job ceiling. Evidence older than24h is
uncertified in new observations. Metadata failure cannot alter old raw history
or enable trading. New identity requires a new instrument/episode; no merging
of pre-certification samples into the certified training cohort.
