# Executive Pump Intelligence

The executive report precedes the existing technical tables and uses the selected
compatible Pump contract and horizon. It states hits, known non-hits and unknowns
before displaying descriptive percentages. Seven observed hits and eight unknown
outcomes in fifteen incomplete observations are reported as those counts, never
as operational success for the entire sample. Each target keeps its own denominator.

Expandable conditions compare the group with its baseline, retaining observations,
episodes, instruments, coverage, pending records, gaps, period and contract.
The group's hit rate uses hits/known; concentration among baseline hits uses
group hits/baseline hits and is labelled separately. Differences are exploratory,
not estimated predictive advantages. Multiple comparisons, correlated episodes,
missing values and the bounded temporal sample remain visible.

Hours use observation start, fixed UTC-03:00, including calendar-date changes.
They display sampled exposure and outcomes without an actionable best/worst-hour
ranking. Indicator splits use the sample median of available observed values,
independent of outcomes. Constant fields show one exact-value group; absent
indicators are counted as missing. These presentation ranges do not alter any
admission threshold. A user can explore a displayed indicator condition using the
existing AND explorer; no strategy, score or order is created.

## Separate Shadow context

A bounded, indexed temporal sample of existing Shadow records includes all statuses.
It refreshes only with a new manual Pump analysis and is stored in the same snapshot.
There is no verified Pump-to-Shadow identity link and no join by symbol/time proximity.
Shadow is presented separately as simulation, never actual profit or Pump SL evidence.

Comparable Shadow cohorts require exact lineage and explicit origin, profile version
and hash, engine version and hash, feature/capture/label/barrier versions, entry-price
contract, exit-policy hash, exchange/timeframe/direction and barrier parameters.
Unverified identities remain excluded with their count visible. Even within an
admissible cohort, a terminal outcome is known only with the recorded resolution
timestamp, the closed-OHLCV nominal first-touch exit contract and consistent barrier
reason/timestamp. Same-candle SL/TP touches retain the conservative recorded convention.
Open or unverifiable outcomes stay unknown, with reasons exposed. No SL adjustment
is recommended from this sample.

The context projects metadata only, uses existing indexes and statement timeout,
and applies a cumulative byte prefix before transferring rows. Pump and Shadow
context share the existing Intelligence read-byte budget; the response reports
their separate and combined byte counts. A failed or budget-limited Shadow read
does not erase Pump data or imply zero SLs. No migrations, collections, jobs,
provider resources, model integration or training inputs are added. The Pump
storage cap and label point/byte/deadline budgets remain unchanged.

## Validation

Regression cases cover the seven-hit/eight-unknown example, distinct concentration
denominators, UTC-03 calendar boundaries, range independence from outcomes,
missing indicators, compatible-contract separation, Shadow lineage rejection,
separate profiles/producers/policies, verified reasons and the shared byte prefix.
Manual snapshot/cache and latest-response protections remain under their existing
tests. Post-deploy verification must include authenticated UI proof of the executive
report and disclosure of data gaps; build success alone is insufficient.
